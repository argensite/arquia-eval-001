ARQUÍA-EVAL-001 v0.2.0 — community protocol candidate

This release introduces a versioned measurement envelope with instrument and input hashes, a separately versioned suite of 28 synthetic boundary cases, an evaluator-owned effect ledger, ten additional deliberately faulty controls, deterministic generated sequences and reproducible counterexamples. The portable JSONL contract includes a standalone candidate that does not inherit the historical engine. Readable reports separate contract violations, observed effects, incomplete evidence and execution cost; there is no global safety score.

Historical v0.1.0, v0.1.1 and v0.1.2, their tags, cases and published results are preserved. The original 29 cases and five faulty controls remain part of acceptance. C29 retains its temporal-only regression. Source packages use a fixed inventory and can reproduce their own bytes. English and Spanish guides, contribution templates and a read-only GitHub Actions matrix are included.

Development and initial local audit: Codex assisting José María Garbini. The native publication process requires an authentic closed ChatGPT review, Qwen execution, isolated before/after regression, full offline acceptance and verification of the final archive. Its separate execution JSON asset binds these project-side receipts to the released commit and ZIP. RSI_RELEASE.json and results/ at the repository root are retained historical v0.1.2 evidence, not receipts for this release.

Local Linux checks cover 29 historical cases, 28 community cases, 32 generated cases with seed 42, all five historical controls and ten community controls. The deny-all control has two inconclusive scenarios, preserved as such, and separate non-error counterexamples. Linux isolation restrictions were exercised; Windows/macOS and other Python versions require their actual CI receipts. A configured workflow is not evidence that it passed.

Limits: synthetic, serial, explicitly declared effect boundary only. The ordinary ACL comparator can pass; no incremental advantage of ARQUÍA has been established. The example is independently implemented from the reference engine but authored within this project. Gepetto-tipiak's linked execution remains an external claim; local reproduction of its critique is not authentication of that execution or proof of independence. Checksums identify bytes, not authorship or signatures. No production-safety certification, universal coverage, distributed-system validation or validation of the 0-1-2-3 theory is asserted.

The authorized native RSI cycle becomes eligible hourly, subject to the existing FIFO, permissions, available evidence and genuine ChatGPT/Qwen receipts. It may wait or reject a proposal. It does not promise an hourly release and never repeats an uncertain external write blindly. No model weights are trained.
